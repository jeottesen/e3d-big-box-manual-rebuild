# BigBox printed parts: handoff to the manual

Nov 2, 2026 · @Jeremy

## What the package is

The package is 72 printed parts, one STEP file each, named after the manual's bill of materials. It is at `/home/claude/workspace/assets/bigbox-printed-parts-package/`.

- `parts/` holds the 72 STEP files. Each is one solid in its own frame, moved so its bounding box sits on the origin and never rotated. Slicers open them directly.
- `manifest.json` and `parts.csv` have one row per file: BOM name, quantity, assembly, manual section, manual URL, printer line, source file, size, volume and SHA-256.
- `SHA256SUMS` checks the files (`sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`). Every file was re-imported after writing and has the expected volume.
- The 10 unnamed parts (alternate designs and transit brackets) were dropped on review. The source assemblies are not included.

## What the manual should do

1. Store the STEP files in the manual's downloads area (suggested `downloads/printed-parts/`), keep the file names, and generate the links from `parts.csv` by script, as the repository's rules require. Do not hand-edit generated pages.
2. Add a STEP link to each printed row of the BOM table. Match on the `name` column of `parts.csv`; its `file` column is the path.
3. Do not store the source assemblies. Link to where they come from on the Downloads page. The owner keeps the originals and can fetch an assembly again if needed.
4. Only `X-AXIS CARRIAGE BEARING CLAMP` has two files: `__dual` and `__regular`. Parts that are the same left and right are one file; the BOM quantity says how many to print.
5. SPOOL-HOLDER OUTER (R) is generated by mirroring (L), which the original files hold once. Say so on the page.
6. Alternates: `Z-AXIS BASE BRACKET LOWER (4 hole)` and `UPPER (4 hole)` are the versions for the newer screw. List them under their standard parts.
