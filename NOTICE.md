## How it was obtained

The wiki is no longer online. Everything here was recovered from archived copies held by the [Internet Archive's Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/).

## Changes made

The recovered material is not identical to the original wiki. Changes include:

- Pages are taken from archived snapshots, mostly 2016–2023, so wording and layout may differ from any single moment on the live wiki.
- Several pages the archive never saved were reconstructed from older versions of the pages that used to contain them. Each is labelled with its source version.
- Many images exist only as thumbnails, and some are missing. Missing images are shown as placeholders.
- Pages were converted from wiki HTML to this repository's formats, and internal links were rewritten.
