# Dual Extruders

Archived 20161105151950 · source: wiki.e3d-online.com/wiki/Dual_Extruders · [archive link](https://web.archive.org/web/20161105151950/http://wiki.e3d-online.com:80/wiki/Dual_Extruders)

\[image not archived: "Dual_head_move.jpg" Caption: "Move."\]

Push the Printhead to the centre of the build space.

\[image not archived: "Dual_head_move_ir.jpg" Caption: "ir."\]

Adjust the height of the IR Sensor Bracket so it is level with the Nozzle.

\[image not archived: "Dual_head_ir_scrwe.jpg" Caption: "Tighten."\]

Secure the IR Sensor.

\[image not archived: "Dual_boot.jpg" Caption: "Booting."\]

Power on your BigBox.

\[image not archived: "Dual_powered.jpg" Caption: "Ready."\]

If all is well you should see three temperature readouts, two nozzles and the heated bed. If you have a MAXTEMP or other error please refer to the support section of the BigBox forum at forum.e3d-online.com *(dead link; [archived copy](https://web.archive.org/web/20240324165937/https://forum.e3d-online.com/))*\
