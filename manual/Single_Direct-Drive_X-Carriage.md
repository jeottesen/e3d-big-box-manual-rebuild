\[image not archived: "Y-AXIS_CARRIAGE_ASSEMBLY.jpg"\]

- Position the previously prepared [Pro & Lite X-Carriage](BigBox_Parts_Preparation.md "BigBox Parts Preparation") onto the bearings. The 'legs' which mount the IR Sensor and Print Fan should be on the back and left respectively as you look at your printer.
- Use the screws shown in the drawing to clamp the bearings. (Thanks' RichRap!). **Do not over-tighten them.**
- Assemble the belt-tensioner (7) with it's fixings (5) and (6). Drop it into the X-Carriage. It should look like the image when you are done.

**Note:** The M3 Grub Screws (8) are used a little later when we install the belt. Do not put them in quite yet, but keep them handy.

**Note:** The Bearings Clamps are designed to fit in one orientation. Check they align correctly before tightening the screws.

## (6.1) X-Carriage Belt

\[image not archived: "X_carriage_belt.jpg" Caption: "Fit the belt."\]

Take one end of the GT2 belt.

Bend it approximately into shape and slide it into the carriage around the belt tensioner and push into place firmly.

\[image not archived: "X_carriage_belt_grub.jpg" Caption: "Insert the Grub."\]

- 1 x M4 8mm Grub Screw (8).

Make sure the belt is pushed all the way into the bottom of the slot.

Screw the 8mm grub screw down into the hole.

**Once it reaches the bottom, do not continue tightening - this may damage the belt**.

\[image not archived: "Feed_x_belt.jpg" Caption: "X-Axis Belt."\]

Feed the belt around the bearings in the X-Axis Idler.

\[image not archived: "X_carriage_belt_motor.jpg" Caption: "X-Axis Belt Threaded onto 20T Pulley"\]

Wind the belt around the X-Motor pulley, then back to the X-Carriage.

\[image not archived: "X_belt_cut.jpg" Caption: "X-Axis Belt being cut to length"\]

Pull the belt taught and cut it to length.

\[image not archived: "Belt_clamped.jpg" Caption: "Finished Belt."\]

- 1 x M4 8mm Grub Screw (8).

Fit and secure the belt with the grub screw.\
